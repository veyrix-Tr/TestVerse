    Questions

    Which exams are covered?

    NEET (UG), JEE Main and JEE Advanced. Each has its own 11-test plan in its current exam pattern.

    What's in the test plan?

    6 part tests, 3 half-syllabus tests and 2 full-syllabus tests for your exam. You can take them whenever you're ready, in any order, though we suggest following the plan.

    What's the difference between the test plan and live tests?

    Test plan tests are available any time. Live tests happen at a fixed time for every student, and you get an all-India rank.

    What if I miss a live test?

    You can still take it afterwards. You'll get your score and analysis, but it won't count towards the all-India rank.
